AmiXcom - installation step by step
===================================

What you need
-------------
- An Amiga with a 68040 or 68060 (a 68020/68030 runs it, slowly). UFO needs
  an FPU; UFO-noFPU does not (68020/68030, LC060/EC060).
- An RTG graphics card with 8-bit (256 colour) screen modes (Picasso96 or
  CyberGraphX). Tested on Warp1260 and Warp4070 (68060), AmigaOS 3.2.
- Fast RAM: 64 MB or more.
- Disk space: about 45 MB for AmiXcom, about 40 MB for the game data and about
  35 MB for the music.
- Your own copy of X-COM: UFO Defense (UFO: Enemy Unknown) for the PC - the
  GOG and Steam versions work. The Amiga version of UFO is not enough on its
  own (its music and sound effects can be added, step 4).


STEP 1 - unpack the archive
---------------------------
In a Shell (Work:Games is only an example; the drawer must exist):

  UnZip AmiXcom-0.xx.zip -d Work:Games

This makes the drawer Work:Games/AmiXcom with the game (UFO, UFO-noFPU),
MakeMusic and the drawers doc (these texts), data and user.


STEP 2 - copy the game data
---------------------------
The X-COM graphics, sounds and maps are not in the archive - they come from
your own game for the PC. Find the directory that holds GEODATA, SOUND and
the others (in the GOG and Steam versions it is the XCOM directory of the
installed game) and copy these directories to the Amiga (network, CF/SD card,
USB stick) into Work:Games/AmiXcom/data/UFO:

  GEODATA GEOGRAPH MAPS ROUTES SOUND TERRAIN UFOGRAPH UNITS
  UFOINTRO (only for the intro - not needed)


STEP 3 - make the music (once)
------------------------------
The music is converted from your own game files (GM.CAT or ADLIB.CAT in
SOUND) to user/music: 14 files, about 35 MB. Without it the game runs
without music. Two ways - pick one:

A) On the Amiga, with MakeMusic (recommended)
   1. Open the AmiXcom drawer and double-click MakeMusic (the loudspeaker
      with notes).
   2. A window shows each tune as it is done. On a 68060 at 100 MHz it
      takes about half a minute (measured: 23 s); the AdLib music (below)
      takes about 15 minutes. A slower Amiga needs correspondingly longer.
      MakeMusic runs on every Amiga the game runs on (68020 or better, no
      FPU needed) and needs about 10 MB of free memory.
   3. Press Return to close the window.
   MakeMusic makes the music the game is set to: "samples" (GM.CAT through
   the port's instruments, the default) or "AdLib" (ADLIB.CAT, the sound of
   the PC's AdLib card) - see Options -> Amiga -> Music source. To make the
   other one, set the Tool Type SOURCE=ADLIB (or SOURCE=SAMPLES) in
   MakeMusic's icon (Icons -> Information), or in a Shell:
     cd Work:Games/AmiXcom
     MakeMusic SOURCE=ADLIB
   Terror from the Deep (its files in data/TFTD): MakeMusic takes the game
   that is active in the game's options, or the Tool Type GAME=TFTD. (Not
   yet tried with the TFTD files.)

B) On a PC, in seconds
   Needs Python 3 and a C++ compiler (g++, e.g. MSYS2 on Windows, or Docker).
   With the AmiXcom sources (github.com/cizar-szymon/Amiga_Game_UFO), the
   unpacked archive in a directory AmiXcom and the X-COM game directory:
     python tools/music/make_music.py --game AmiXcom --sound "C:\GOG Games\UFO Defense\XCOM"
   (add --source adlib for the AdLib music), then copy AmiXcom/user/music to
   the Amiga as Work:Games/AmiXcom/user/music. The sampled music comes out the
   same, byte for byte, as on the Amiga; the AdLib music is the same music
   (same length and loudness), only single sample values may differ a
   little (the emulated AdLib chip computes some tables in floating point).

The game itself does not convert the music: with nothing in user/music
its loading screen says "NO MUSIC YET" and the game plays without music.


STEP 4 - the Amiga UFO's music and sound effects (optional)
-----------------------------------------------------------
From your own Amiga UFO disks, copy to Work:Games/AmiXcom/data/UFO_AMIGA the files
  Defend.mus EnBase.mus Geo.mus Inter.mus Lose.mus Mars.mus NewMars.mus
  Story.mus Win.mus UFO.bank   and the directories TacticalFX GeoScapeFX
then choose Options -> Amiga -> Music = Amiga and Sound effects = Amiga.


STEP 5 - start the game
-----------------------
Double-click UFO (or UFO-noFPU). The icon gives the game its 1 MB stack;
from a Shell: Stack 1000000, then UFO. Options -> Video: "WARP RTG 320x200 /
640x480" plays the battles on a 640x480 screen.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- The game says data is missing: the directories of step 2 are not in
  Work:Games/AmiXcom/data/UFO.
- No music: step 3 was not done, or Options -> Amiga -> Music is off.
  user/music/source.txt says what the music was made from.
- MakeMusic says "No GM.CAT or ADLIB.CAT": SOUND is missing in data/UFO.
- An error in the game: press F10 while it is on the screen, then send the
  files user/xcom1/bugNNN.* with sdlmini.log, user/openxcom.log and the
  release number (main menu, "Version RTG 0.xx").
