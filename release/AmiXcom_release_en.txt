AmiXcom 0.86 beta - OpenXcom (UFO: Enemy Unknown / X-COM) for the classic Amiga
=============================================================================

Release 0.86, 24.10.2026. "0" means beta; official releases will be 1.xx.
The number is shown in the main menu ("Version RTG 0.86") and by the Shell
command "Version UFO".

REPORTING A BUG
---------------
Press F10 in the game where the problem is on screen. It writes
user/xcom1/bugNNN.png (the screen), bugNNN.txt (what the game was doing) and
bugNNN.sav (the saved game). Send those three files together with
sdlmini.log, user/openxcom.log and the version number. Help takes a plain
screenshot.

WHAT IS IN THE ARCHIVE
----------------------
UFO            the game for 68040/68060 WITH an FPU (the one to use on a Warp)
UFO-noFPU      the same game without FPU code: 68020/68030, LC060/EC060
data/          OpenXcom's own data, translations, music instruments
data/UFO/      empty - your X-COM game data goes here (see below)
data/TFTD/     empty - Terror from the Deep data, optional
data/UFO_AMIGA empty - files of the Amiga release of UFO, optional

No X-COM game data is included: you need your own copy of the PC game
(GOG and Steam copies work).

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- RTG graphics card with an 8-bit screen mode (Picasso96 / CyberGraphX).
  Tested on Warp1260 and Warp4070 (68060) with AmigaOS 3.2.
- 68020 or better; a 68060 is recommended. UFO needs an FPU, UFO-noFPU not.
- Lots of Fast RAM: 64 MB or more.
- Disk: ~43 MB unpacked (17 MB archive), ~40 MB for the game data, ~36 MB for the
  PC music converted to disk at the first start.

INSTALLING
----------
1. Unpack the archive anywhere, e.g. Work:  ->  Work:AmiXcom/
2. Copy from your PC game the folders
     GEODATA GEOGRAPH MAPS ROUTES SOUND TERRAIN UFOGRAPH UNITS
   into data/UFO/. Add UFOINTRO too for the intro.
3. Optional - the Amiga UFO's own music and sound effects: copy from your
   Amiga UFO disks into data/UFO_AMIGA/ the files
     Defend.mus EnBase.mus Geo.mus Inter.mus Lose.mus Mars.mus NewMars.mus
     Story.mus Win.mus UFO.bank   and the folders TacticalFX GeoScapeFX
   Then pick MUSIC = Amiga and SOUND EFFECTS = Amiga in Options -> Amiga.
4. Double-click UFO (or UFO-noFPU). The icon gives the game its 1 MB stack;
   from a Shell: Stack 1000000, then UFO.
   The first start takes longer (the PC music is converted to disk once).

KEYS
----
F10            bug report (see above)
Help           screenshot
Esc / mouse    skips the intro
Top right 2x2 pixels of the screen: click to flip to the Workbench.

OPTIONS -> AMIGA
----------------
Map reveal, Split movement calculation, Music (Off / Amiga / PC game),
Music source (Samples / AdLib), Sound effects (PC game / Amiga), Language
from Workbench, Show FPS. On top: the Warp card, its firmware and CPU clock
(from SYS:C/WarpTool, if present).
Options -> Video: "WARP RTG 320x200 / 640x400" and "... / 640x480": the
Geoscape on a 320x200 screen, battles native at 640x400 or 640x480.

CHANGES SINCE 0.9.14 (author's last release)
---------------------------------------------
Speed (measured on Warp1260 / Warp4070, 68060):
- Battles at 640x400: standing 26 -> 50 fps, scrolling 14 -> 37 fps,
  soldiers walking ~15 -> ~28 fps; the stop on every bullet hit
  0.65 -> 0.28 s; enemy reactions after each step 25-82 ms -> under 10 ms.
- Geoscape: 14 -> 49 fps while time runs; globe redraw 150 -> 40 ms.
- 1 DAY speed: a quiet day 2.6 -> 0.3 s, a craft flying 5.5 -> 0.85 s.
- Saving: Geoscape autosave 8.3 -> 0.14 s; loading a save 4.9 -> 0.6 s;
  start ~17 s shorter (new memory allocator).
- Code tuned for the 68060 (no trapped 64-bit multiplies).
New:
- The UFO intro plays, with its original sound, WHILE the game loads;
  Esc or a click shows the title picture with "LOADING nn %".
- Native battles at 640x400 and 640x480 (Warp entries in Options -> Video).
- Music and sound effects from the Amiga release of UFO, next to the PC ones.
- Load/save progress box; F10 bug report; Help screenshot.
- Options -> Amiga in Polish too; Show FPS (off by default).
- No title bar; the screen flips to the Workbench from its top right corner.
Fixed:
- Flickering battle panel and windows; message boxes left on the screen.
- Hangs and Gurus (a freed buffer after a resolution change, Ctrl-C, the
  double buffer); map pictures out of step with the game in several cases.
- F12 / screenshots closed the game; 10 s of black screen after loading;
  the intro did not play; intro sounds were cut.
- No console window on the Workbench when started from the icon.
