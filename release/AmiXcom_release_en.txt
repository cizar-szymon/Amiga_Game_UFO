AmiXcom 0.106 beta - OpenXcom (UFO: Enemy Unknown / X-COM) for the Amiga with a Warp card
=============================================================================

Release 0.106, 2.11.2026. "0" means beta; official releases will be 1.xx.
The number is shown in the main menu ("Version RTG 0.106") and by the Shell
command "Version UFO".

REPORTING A BUG
---------------
Press F10 in the game where the problem is on screen. It writes
user/xcom1/bugNNN.png (the screen), bugNNN.txt (what the game was doing) and
bugNNN.sav (the saved game). Send those three files together with
sdlmini.log, user/openxcom.log and the version number. Help takes a plain
screenshot.

WHAT IS IN THE ARCHIVE
----------------------
UFO            the game for 68040/68060 WITH an FPU (the one to use on a Warp)
UFO-noFPU      the same game without FPU code: 68020/68030, LC060/EC060
MakeMusic      makes the game's music from your X-COM files (Install_en.txt, step 3)
Install_en.txt installation step by step (also _pl, _de)
data/          OpenXcom's own data, translations, music instruments
data/UFO/      empty - your X-COM game data goes here (see below)
data/TFTD/     empty - Terror from the Deep data, optional
data/UFO_AMIGA empty - files of the Amiga release of UFO, optional

No X-COM game data is included: you need your own copy of the PC game
(GOG and Steam copies work).

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Made for the Amiga 500 / 1200 / 3000 / 4000 with a Warp card (Warp 560,
  1240, 1260, 1260 Extreme, 4070); tested on an A1200 with a Warp 1260
  Extreme and an A4000 with a Warp 4070 (68060, AmigaOS 3.2). The Warp's fast
  graphics card gives the speeds below; other 68040/68060 Amigas with an RTG
  card run the game, slower.
- RTG graphics card with an 8-bit screen mode (Picasso96 / CyberGraphX).
- UFO needs a 68040/68060 with FPU; UFO-noFPU runs on a 68020/68030, LC060.
- Lots of Fast RAM: 64 MB or more.
- Disk: ~43 MB unpacked (16 MB archive), ~40 MB for the game data, ~35 MB for the
  PC music (made once by MakeMusic, Install_en.txt, step 3).

INSTALLING
----------
1. Unpack the archive anywhere, e.g. in a Shell
   UnZip AmiXcom-0.106.zip -d Work:Games  ->  Work:Games/AmiXcom/
2. Copy from your PC game the folders
     GEODATA GEOGRAPH MAPS ROUTES SOUND TERRAIN UFOGRAPH UNITS
   into data/UFO/. Add UFOINTRO too for the intro.
3. Optional - the Amiga UFO's own music and sound effects: copy from your
   Amiga UFO disks into data/UFO_AMIGA/ the files
     Defend.mus EnBase.mus Geo.mus Inter.mus Lose.mus Mars.mus NewMars.mus
     Story.mus Win.mus UFO.bank   and the folders TacticalFX GeoScapeFX
   Then pick MUSIC = Amiga and SOUND EFFECTS = Amiga in Options -> Amiga.
4. Double-click UFO (or UFO-noFPU). The icon gives the game its 1 MB stack;
   from a Shell: Stack 1000000, then UFO.
   MakeMusic makes the PC music once (Install_en.txt, step 3); the game
   itself does not convert it.

KEYS
----
F10            bug report (see above)
Help           screenshot
Esc / mouse    skips the intro
Top right 2x2 pixels of the screen: click to flip to the Workbench.

OPTIONS -> AMIGA
----------------
Map reveal, Split movement calculation, Music (Off / Amiga / PC game),
Music source (Samples / AdLib), Sound effects (PC game / Amiga), Language
from Workbench, Show FPS. On top: the Warp card, its firmware and CPU clock
(from SYS:C/WarpTool, if present).
Options -> Video: "WARP RTG 320x200 / 640x400" and "... / 640x480": the
Geoscape on a 320x200 screen, battles native at 640x400 or 640x480.

SPEED (Warp 1260, 68060 at 100 MHz)
-----------------------------------
- Battles at 640x400 / 640x480: about 50 fps standing, 33-37 fps
  scrolling, 28 fps while soldiers walk; a large unit (sectopod, reaper,
  cyberdisc, the X-COM tanks) thinks for about 2 s at most in an alien turn.
- Geoscape: about 49 fps while time runs; a quiet 1 DAY step 0.3 s.
- Saving: 0.14 s on the Geoscape, under half a second in a battle; loading
  a save about 6 s; from the icon to the menu about 30 s.

RELEASE HISTORY
---------------
0.106 (2.11.2026)
- The release is a ZIP archive (UnZip on the Amiga) and lives in the
  repository, releases/amiga; the texts have icons that open in MultiView.
- The save list shows the date and time of every save, from the Amiga's
  clock.
- The game loads on a 320x200 screen (the Geoscape's size) whatever screen
  the last game ended on; battles open at 640x400 or 640x480 as chosen.
- A save made with F10 while a unit is moving loads with the unit standing
  where it was.
- The battle picture stays exact under a tank's 2x2 cursor and behind a
  soldier who has just walked.

0.100 (2.11.2026)
- MakeMusic (its icon in the AmiXcom drawer) makes the PC music from your
  X-COM files: about half a minute on a 68060 at 100 MHz (the AdLib music
  about 15 minutes), with a progress window. The game starts without
  making music; without music the loading screen says "NO MUSIC YET". On a
  PC the same is done by tools/music/make_music.py of the sources. Terror
  from the Deep: Tool Type GAME=TFTD (not yet tried with the TFTD files).
- Installation step by step: Install_en.txt, Install_pl.txt, Install_de.txt.
- Scrolling the battle map stops smoothly; the "Hidden Movement" screen
  stays on the screen without being redrawn, which keeps alien and
  civilian turns short.
- Every new window (e.g. "Abort Mission") appears whole at once.

0.86 (24.10.2026) - the first release of this fork
- Native battles at 640x400 and 640x480 (Warp entries in Options -> Video);
  the battle picture lives in the graphics card's memory and the card's
  blitter scrolls it.
- The UFO intro plays, with its original sound, while the game loads; Esc
  or a click shows the title picture with "LOADING nn %".
- Music and sound effects from the Amiga release of UFO, next to the PC
  ones.
- Load/save progress box; F10 bug report; Help screenshot.
- Options -> Amiga in Polish too; Show FPS (off by default).
- No title bar; the screen flips to the Workbench from its top right corner.
- A program started from its icon opens no console window.
- Code tuned for the 68040/68060; its own memory allocator.
